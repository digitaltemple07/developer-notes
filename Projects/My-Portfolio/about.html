ABOUT KEN

Hey my name is Imafe Kennedy Oisamoren, I am from Otuo in Owan East Local Government Area, South-South Senetorial District of Edo State Nigeria. I am a passionate and driven individual with a strong interest in technology, business, and education.

I am the founder of the Neo Black Consciousness Movement (NBCM). Neo Black Consciousness Movement is an Africa Educational and Civic Movement. Dedicated in promoting historical understanding, critical thinking, ethical leadership, Good Governance, Human Right and responsible citizenship across Africa and Africa diaspora.

I am a digital creator, The founder of Ken Digital Lab, where we teach entrepreneurs how to build, manage and grow profitable e-commerce web store without Hiring a developer. i am strategic entrepreneur and a lifelong learner who enjoys building products that solve real-world problems.

I am the founder of BRAINWAVE SCENT, where we explore what African customers want from fragrance—and translating those desires, environments, emotions, and identities into original scents. I'm passionate about combining technology, business and education to create useful experiences that help people and businesses grow.

What I'm Learning

I'm currently strengthening my knowledge of HTML, CSS and JavaScript. I'm also learning Git and GitHub.

My Goal

My goal is to become a full-stack web developer and continue building useful digital products.
